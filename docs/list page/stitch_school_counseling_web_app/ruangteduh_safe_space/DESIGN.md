## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for a school counseling, emotional wellness, and student venting platform. The brand personality embodies empathy, reassurance, warmth, and unhurried emotional safety. It speaks directly to students aged 12–18 who navigate academic stress, social friction, anxiety, and personal growth, as well as school counselors (Guru BK) and peer listeners (Teman Sebaya).

The visual movement pairs **Tactile Warm Minimalism** with **Calming Organic Softness**. Rather than clinical healthcare sterility or overly playful gamification, the interface prioritizes a grounded, paper-soft sanctuary feel. Tactile, pillowed containers, atmospheric breathing room, and whisper-quiet outlines remove emotional overwhelm. The experience feels like opening an intimate, private journal or entering a quiet, sunlit counseling alcove—grounded, safe, and confidential.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep biophilic greens, comforting botanical mid-tones, gentle lavenders, and sun-warmed pastels that avoid triggering clinical anxiety or sensory fatigue.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#2D6A4F` - Forest Canopy)**: Anchor for focal interactive elements, confirmed actions, and grounding typography. Communicates resilience, trust, and professional support.
- **Primary Light / Container (`#D8F3DC` - Mint Leaf Whisper)**: Low-stress background fills for active states, empathetic callouts, and conversational bubbles.
- **Primary Mid (`#52B788` - Sage Grove)**: Expressive accents, active tabs, progress bars, and peer badges.
- **Secondary (`#6B6487` - Calming Lavender Slate)**: Introduces introspection, gentleness, and non-judgmental space. Used for counselor private notes, reflective questions, and peer support indicators.
- **Secondary Container (`#EAE6F2` - Heather Mist)**: Soft tinted backdrop for personal journal surfaces and reflective prompts.
- **Tertiary (`#E08738` - Warm Amber Hearth)**: Applied exclusively for statuses requiring attention without inducing panic—such as "Menunggu Respon" (Pending Response) or gentle crisis helpline reminders.
- **Tertiary Container (`#FDF1E2` - Warm Chamomile)**: Background token for attention notices, gentle reminders, and waiting states.
- **Neutral / Surface (`#F8FAF9` - Steamed Rice/Creamy Paper)**: The fundamental canvas. A warm, non-glare off-white that feels tactile and unhurried.
- **Neutral Variant / Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Lily)**: Raised card surface, input field canvas, and clean modal dialogs.
- **Text & Foreground (`#1F2923` - Deep Spruce Charcoal)**: High-legibility text with natural pigment warmth, replacing harsh pure black.
- **Muted Foreground (`#56625B` - River Rock)**: Supporting subtext, timestamps, and secondary metadata.

### Functional Status Tokens
- **Anonim (Anonymous)**: Slate stone pill (`#F1F5F3`, text `#425248`).
- **Terbuka (Public/Open)**: Soft sky teal pill (`#E0F2EE`, text `#1B5E4F`).
- **Ke Guru BK (To Counselor)**: Muted lavender slate (`#ECE9F5`, text `#4E446B`).
- **Ke Teman Sebaya (To Peer)**: Soft leaf green (`#E3F7E8`, text `#215934`).
- **Menunggu Respon (Pending)**: Soft apricot amber (`#FDF2E2`, text `#8A4A0A`).
- **Sudah Dibalas (Answered)**: Sage moss glow (`#E4F5EB`, text `#1F683D`).

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the singular typographic voice across display, body, and UI labeling. Its humanist architecture, rounded terminal geometry, and balanced open counters introduce an instinctively friendly and approachable cadence without sliding into childlike informality.

- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`)**: Feature tight, gentle letter tracking and generous vertical spacing, preventing dense, intimidating block headlines when students share sensitive thoughts.
- **Reading Content (`body-lg`, `body-md`)**: Designed for emotional consumption. Line heights are calibrated generously (1.6x–1.65x) to maintain a serene, uncrowded cadence when reading personal stories, teacher reflections, or long anonymous submissions.
- **Labels & Badges (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`)**: Semi-bold to bold weights maintain crisp legibility at small scale within pill badges, timestamp markers, and safety cues.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an intentional **calming fluid grid** that caps content widths to prevent cognitive fatigue. Personal counseling platforms become overwhelming when stretched wide across monitor screens; therefore, text feeds, confession threads, and counselor conversation windows are constrained within a maximum width of `720px` to `840px` (reading-optimal width), even within wide viewports.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column system with `1rem` (16px) outer page margins and `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Bottom navigation bar anchors key safe actions: Curhat Cepat (Quick Vent), Ruang Tanya, and Buku Harian (Private Diary).
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Split panels allow quick transitions between reading curated peer answers and submitting confidential questions.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. A calm side rail (navigation & quick emergency crisis resources) leaves the central 8 columns dedicated to clean, non-distracting reading and writing environments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tonal Layering with Sage Ambient Diffusion** rather than sharp directional drop shadows. The intent is to keep interfaces peaceful, soft, and organic.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Elevation 0)**: `#F8FAF9` (Off-white paper cream). The calm foundation where reading occurs.
- **Resting Cards (Elevation 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft dual shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02);`
  - Border: 1px subtle outline `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive / Floating State (Elevation 2)**: For hovered story cards, chat composer docks, and dropdown menus:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.03);`
  - Border: 1px outline in `#52B788` at 20% opacity.
- **Modals & Empathetic Overlays (Elevation 3)**: Modals and crisis intervention sheets:
  - Backdrop: `#1F2923` with 30% opacity and `blur(8px)` backdrop filter to softly defocus underlying stress while keeping context.
  - Container: `#FFFFFF` with `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, welcoming curves to physically eliminate sharp corners that feel harsh or punitive. 

- **Pills & Status Badges**: Full radius (`9999px` / capsule) for badges, tags, and category chips. This shape feels tactile, friendly, and gentle to touch.
- **Story Cards & Input Boxes**: Large rounded corners (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px) to present posts as warm physical note cards or supportive journal sheets.
- **Buttons & Dialogs**: `1rem` (16px) for standard interactive buttons, producing a soft pebble-like touch target.
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Sheets**: Extra large top rounding (`rounded-2xl`, 2rem / 32px) on mobile bottom sheets to embrace the hand naturally.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Kirim Curhat", "Mulai Konseling")**: Background `#2D6A4F`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `1rem` (16px), padding `14px 24px`. On hover: `#23533E` with subtle lift (`translateY(-1px)`). No sharp edges.
- **Secondary ("Simpan Draft", "Batal")**: Background `#D8F3DC` with text `#2D6A4F`. Light, low-friction, welcoming.
- **Ghost ("Lihat Selengkapnya")**: Transparent background with text `#2D6A4F` and subtle hover fill `#EBF7EE`.
- **Crisis / Bantuan Cepat ("Hubungi Guru BK Langsung")**: Soft warm crimson-amber pill background `#FDE8E8` with `#9B1C1C` text and gentle pulse indicator.

### Status Badges & Chips
Badges use pill styling (`rounded-full`, padding `6px 12px`, typography `label-sm` or `label-md`) with soft pastel fills and deep harmonic text:
- **Anonim**: Fill `#EEF2F0`, text `#475850`, left icon: subtle lock or mask avatar.
- **Terbuka**: Fill `#E3F3EF`, text `#1B5E4F`.
- **Ke Guru BK**: Fill `#ECE8F6`, text `#534675`, left icon: graduation/heart icon.
- **Ke Teman Sebaya**: Fill `#E5F6EB`, text `#21633A`, left icon: double smile or hands icon.
- **Menunggu Respon**: Fill `#FEF3E2`, text `#964F06`, left dot: warm amber animated pulse.
- **Sudah Dibalas**: Fill `#E4F5EB`, text `#1B5E3B`, left icon: check circle icon.

### Input Fields & Story Composer
- **Venting Text Area**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E1E8E4`, rounded `1.25rem` (20px), padding `18px`. Generous internal padding and placeholder text written with reassurance (*"Tuliskan apa yang sedang membebani pikiranmu di sini... Ruang ini aman untukmu"* in `#8C9992`).
- **Focus State**: Border `#52B788`, subtle ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.18)`, no harsh black borders.
- **Anonymous Mode Toggle**: An empathetic, oversized toggle switch with soft cloud/mask icon feedback.

### Cards (Curhat Card & Counselor Profile)
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAF9`.
- **Corner Radius**: `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Padding**: `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Layout Structure**:
  1. Top header row: Badge category (e.g., *Ke Guru BK*) + Status badge (e.g., *Sudah Dibalas*) + Timestamp.
  2. Content body: Title in `title-md`, followed by curated snippet in `body-md`.
  3. Footer: Interaction pills (e.g., "3 Tanggapan Menenangkan", "Kirim Pelukan Virtual / Dukungan").

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes and radio discs have `20px` dimension with soft `6px` radius (checkbox) or circular shape (radio).
- Active state uses `#2D6A4F` fill with crisp white internal check or center dot. 
- Touch targets expanded to minimum `44px` with clear, accessible tap areas.

### Empathy Extras: Mood Tracker Pill Bar
- Horizontal scrollable or segmented selector composed of mood chips: *Cemas, Lelah, Netral, Lega, Bersyukur*.
- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` border `1px solid #E3ECE6`.
- Selected state: `#D8F3DC` background with `#2D6A4F` border and text, accompanied by calm, hand-drawn vector expression motifs.
